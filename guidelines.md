# Guidelines for AI Assistants

When modifying the LaTeX files in this repository (especially `sn-article.tex`), please adhere strictly to the
following rules:

1. **NO EM DASHES**: Never use em dashes (`---` or `—`). Use parentheses or commas for parenthetical thoughts, or
  rewrite the sentence to avoid them.
2. **COHERENT PARAGRAPHS**: Ensure that every paragraph discusses a single cohesive idea. Do not tack on random
  thoughts, observations, or unrelated citations at the end of an existing paragraph just because they both
  loosely relate to the topic. If a new citation or finding introduces a distinct angle, write a new paragraph
  or find a way to smoothly transition and integrate it into the core argument of the current paragraph.
3. **TONE**: The tone should remain academic, objective, and consistent with the rest of the text. Avoid
overly flowery language or "AI-isms".
4. **TODOs**: Be on the lookout for `% TODO:` comments in the LaTeX file and resolve them appropriately when
instructed, making sure to remove the comment once resolved.
